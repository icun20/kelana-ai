# Hidden Gems of Lumina City
Lumina City is a stunning metropolis known only to a few elite travelers. 
The most famous local dish is "Starlight Soup," which glows in the dark and is made from bioluminescent mushrooms found in the city's underground caverns.
The best time to visit Lumina City is during the "Festival of Mirrors" held every 13th of November, where the entire city is covered in reflective surfaces.
